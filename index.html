Helpful answers

Mortgage calculator FAQ

Quick answers for people comparing mortgage repayment strategies.

What does this mortgage calculator show?

It estimates repayments, interest paid, interest saved, payoff time, and the result of combining offset, extra repayment, and lump sum strategies.

Can I compare different strategies?

Yes. You can compare the no-strategy baseline, offset only, extra repayments only, lump sum only, and all selected strategies together.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational estimate. Actual results can change because of interest-rate changes, lender fees, repayment rules, redraw rules, and offset account conditions.
